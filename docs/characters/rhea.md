# Rhea: The Rushdown

A kickboxer who never stops coming. Fast on her feet, quick to start every attack, but
lighter hitting and easier to knock around than anyone else.

## Look

- Female base body, auburn hair in buns.
- Red sports crop top, black fight shorts, white hand wraps.
- Alternate costume (mirror matches): blue top, white shorts, dark wraps, black hair.

## Stats

| HP | Walk | Dash | Throw | Damage | Startup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 960 | 2.6 | 7.5 | 100 | ×0.95 | −1 frame |

Knockback ×0.9 and hitstop −1, so her pressure is tight; weight 0.85, so she flies
further when hit.

## Moves

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 6HP | Rushing Hook | Lunging hook to the body (mid). |
| 6LK | Step Kick | Lunging kick that cancels into 6HP or LP. |
| 236K | Gale Slide | Feet-first slide: hits low, ducks under highs, knocks down. |
| 623K | Crescent Rise | Invincible rising kick (frames 1–6). Launches. |
| 236236K | Tempest Kicks (super) | Seven-hit kick flurry into a Crescent Rise finisher. |

Plus the shared base moves (see the [roster overview](README.md)).

## Game plan

Dash in, stay in, and mix blocking opponents up between throws, Step Kick strings and
the low Gale Slide.

## Presentation

- Victory: hip cocked, hand on hip, pointing at the camera; or the fist pump.
- Voice: "Female Hurt Grunts & Groans" by AuraVoice.
- Character select routine: Tempest Kicks with the finisher, Gale Slide, Rushing Hook,
  Crescent Rise, point, with only short guard pauses in between.

## CPU style: Rushdown

Prefers 0.9 m, +0.25 aggression, dashes, jumps in and throws a lot, and keeps the
pressure on a blocking opponent (pressure 0.75).
