# Kenji: The Balanced Fighter

The first fighter and the roster's all-rounder. A karateka who controls the screen with
a fireball, shuts down jumps with an invincible uppercut, and has a clean answer for
everything without being the best at any one thing.

## Look

- Male base body, light skin, side-parted dark hair.
- White karate gi with a deep V, black belt, red headband.
- Alternate costume (mirror matches): navy gi, brown belt, white headband, brown hair.

## Stats

| HP | Walk | Dash | Throw | Damage | Startup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1000 | 2.0 | 6.0 | 120 | ×1.05 | ±0 |

## Moves

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 6HP | Advancing Straight | A straight that lunges forward (12f). |
| 6LK | Snap Kick | The fastest kick in the game (5f); cancels into HP or 6HP. |
| 236P | Ki Blast | Fireball, 6.5 m/s: slow enough to walk behind, fast enough to zone with. |
| 623P | Rising Dragon | Invincible rising uppercut (frames 1–7). Launches; very punishable on a miss (+16 landing frames). |
| 236236P | Dragon Barrage (super) | Six-hit punch flurry into a Rising Dragon finisher. |

Plus the shared base moves (see the [roster overview](README.md)).

## Game plan

Zone from range with Ki Blast, anti-air whoever jumps over it with Rising Dragon, and
use Advancing Straight to close the gap when the opponent sits still.

## Presentation

- Victory: fist-in-palm salute and bow, or the fist pump.
- Voice: "Male Grunt/Yelling sounds" by HaelDB, voice 1.
- Character select routine: Ki Blast with a flying orb, Rising Dragon, jab → straight,
  bow.

## CPU style: Zoner

Prefers 2.6 m, throws plenty of fireballs (about 16 per 30 s on Hard), backs off when
crowded and anti-airs with Rising Dragon.
