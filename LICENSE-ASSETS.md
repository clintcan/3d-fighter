## What this covers

Assets created for this project (they are marked **CC BY 4.0** in [assets/CREDITS.md](assets/CREDITS.md)):

- **Stages:** the generated Boxing Ring, Dojo, Rooftop and Temple scenes (`scenes/stages/`), the crowds, and the rooftop night-sky shader.
- **Animations:** the generated fight, special-move and victory animations (`assets/animations/fight_anims.res`).
- **Art:** the character portraits, the key art and boot splash, and the stage-select thumbnails (`assets/ui/`).
- **Sounds:** the synthesized swing, energy-blast and super-flash sounds (`assets/audio/sfx/swing_*.wav`, `energy.wav`, `super.wav`).
